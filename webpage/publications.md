# <img src="../assets/images/openmoji/1F4C3.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"/> Publications

You will find my most recent publications by searching on
[ArXiv](https://arxiv.org/search/?searchtype=author&query=Witzany%2C+V),
[InspireHEP](https://inspirehep.net/authors/1436457), or
[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=6ErvxkMAAAAJ).

The list below is updated to **October 2026**. Only *published* peer-reviewed works are
included in reverse chronological order; conference proceedings and preprints are omitted.

---

- Polcar, L. & Witzany, V. (2025).  
  **Toward relativistic inspirals into black holes surrounded by matter.**  
  *Phys. Rev. D*, 112(10), 104003. [arXiv:2507.15720](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.15720)  
  *Relativistic treatment of orbital dynamics and gravitational-wave driven inspirals near a black hole embedded in an axisymmetric matter distribution.*

- Skoupý, V., Piovano, G. A. & Witzany, V. (2025).

  *Phys. Rev. D*, 112(12), 124054. [arXiv:2506.20726](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.20726)  
  *Self-force driven inspirals of spinning compact objects on spherical orbits around a Kerr black hole, including spin–orbit effects on the gravitational waveforms.*

- Skoupý, V. & Witzany, V. (2025).

  *Phys. Rev. Lett.*, 134(17), 171401. [arXiv:2411.16855](https://arxiv.org/abs/2411.16855)

  *Phys. Rev. D*, 111(4), 044009. [arXiv:2410.05769](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.05769)

- Afshordi, N., et al. (LISA Consortium Waveform Working Group, incl. Witzany, V.) (2025).  
  **Waveform modelling for the Laser Interferometer Space Antenna.**  
  *Living Rev. Relativ.*, 28(1), 9. [arXiv:2311.01300](https://arxiv.org/abs/2311.01300)
