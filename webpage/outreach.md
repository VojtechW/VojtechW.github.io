# <img src="../assets/images/openmoji/1F5E3.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"/> Outreach

I like talking about science from a generalist point of view. I think it brings
something to the people who listen but also forces me to take on fresh perspectives.

For a period of time I contributed regularly to Physics Stack Exchange
(see [my profile](https://physics.stackexchange.com/users/52394/void)).

## <img src="../assets/images/openmoji/1F399.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"/> Public talks

I occasionally give popular-science talks at high schools and other public venues,
mostly in Czech. A recorded talk is available on YouTube:

- [**Černé díry, gravitační vlny a vesmírná mise LISA** (Czech)](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GysjONMr8pk) — Recorded talk at the Open Day of the Faculty of Mathematics and Physics, Charles University, 27 November 2025.

## <img src="../assets/images/openmoji/1F5DE.svg" alt="" aria-hidden="true"/> Articles for *Respekt*

I semi-regularly write popular science articles for the Czech magazine
[*Respekt*](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Respekt):

- [*Velké oko v kosmu* (The big eye in space), 3.5. 2015](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2015/19/velke-oko-v-kosmu) — A retrospective for the 25 years of the Hubble space telescope
- [*Čekání na velký třesk* (Waiting for the big bang), 31.5. 2015](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2015/23/cekani-na-velky-tresk) — Article summarising the expectations from the second run of LHC
- [*Když v kosmu hřmí* (When the cosmic thunder claps), 13.2. 2016](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2016/7/kdyz-v-kosmu-hrmi) — About the announced first measurement of gravitational waves
- [*Na lovu černé tečky* (Hunting for the black spot), 20.5. 2017](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2017/21/na-lovu-cerne-tecky) — About the Event Horizon Telescope collaboration and its prospects
- [*Jak vzniká zlato. Vědci zaznamenali srážku dvou neutronových hvězd* (Where does gold come from. Scientists have observed a neutron star collision), 16.10. 2017](https://www.respekt.cz/spolecnost/jak-vznika-zlato-vedci-zaznamenali-srazku-dvou-neutronovych-hvezd) — On the first announced measurement and observations of the binary neutron star merger
- [*Jak mě Stephen Hawking přivedl k vědě* (How Stephen Hawking brought me to science), 14.3. 2018](https://www.respekt.cz/spolecnost/jak-me-stephen-hawking-privedl-k-vede) — A personal story about the impact of Hawking's *A Brief History of Time* on my life
- [*Na vlnách podivna* (On stranger waves), 15.4. 2019](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2019/16/na-vlnach-podivna) — About the announced EHT observations of M87 and their implications
- [*Na dřeň přírodních zákonů* (To the hard core of nature), 10.11. 2019](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2019/46/na-dren-prirodnich-zakonu) — On quantum computing and the implications of the quantum supremacy claim by Google
- [*Tygr jménem magnetar* (The cosmic tiger, magnetar), 15.11. 2020](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2020/47/tygr-jmenem-magnetar) — On the first association of a fast radio burst with a galactic source
- [*UFO poblíž Chicaga* (The UFO near Chicago), 23.5. 2021](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2021/21/ufo-pobliz-chicaga) — Article about the muon magnetic moment anomaly (g-2) announced by Fermilab and its implications
- [*Stín něžného obra* (The shadow of the gentle giant), 15.5. 2022](https://www.respekt.cz/tydenik/2022/20/stin-nezneho-obra) — About the first "photo" of the supermassive black hole in the centre of our Milky Way galaxy
